# Natural-text survey sample

`survey_v1.jsonl` holds 200 sentences from 40 PLOS ONE research articles. Each row carries the article's DOI, title, authors, and licence URL. All source articles are published under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 licence (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/); the sentences are reproduced unchanged apart from removal of inline citation markers and whitespace normalisation.

Method and counts: `reports/natural_survey_v1.md`. Tool: `tools/natural_snapshot.py`.
